Civic Process

How GreenCity Works

A seamless 3-step transparent pathway turning citizen observations into verified civic solutions.

1

Spot & Document

Take a photo of any environmental hazard, select the category, and drop an exact pin on our interactive city map.

2

City Triage & Action

Authorized municipal inspectors review the report, assign response teams, and update the status in real time to "In Progress".

3

Verified Resolution

The issue is resolved with public notes and metrics logged, contributing directly to your community's UN SDG impact score.
